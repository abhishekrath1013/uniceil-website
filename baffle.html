Baffle System

Uniceil Baffles effortlessly tuck away utilities behind a flawless finish. The result? Walls, ceilings and facades that are sharper, cleaner and smarter.

Get a Quote Download spec sheet

Why It’s Specified

  • Three configurations, one system

    Linear, In-Fill and open Baffle blades share the same carrier, fixing and finish logic.

  • Tool-free access

    Linear and Baffle lengths lift out for above-ceiling service without full demolition.

  • Monolithic option

    In-Fill’s concealed click-lock interlock reads as one continuous plane where the profile itself should disappear.

  • Built-in lighting channel

    The patented LED channel is extruded into the profile, flush with the surface — no visible housing or driver box.

  • One finish library

    Matte, gloss and wood-look laminate finishes, co-extruded through every configuration, so colour never sits as a coating.

  • Matched trims and connectors

    Corner trims, end caps and carriers share one range across Linear, In-Fill and Baffle.
